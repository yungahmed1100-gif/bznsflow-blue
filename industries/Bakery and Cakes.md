# Bakery and Cakes — Hasib dashboard

## Purpose

Give the owner one simple operating view for custom cakes, daily bakery production, ingredient stock, waste, and margin. Keep the existing Hasib tabs: Today, Chats, Orders, Stock, Money, Customers, and Settings.

## Tabs

### Today

- Orders waiting for owner action.
- Money received today and this month.
- Food cost, prime cost, waste, and stock variance.
- Expiring and expired dated stock for the next seven days.
- Layla activity and customer questions.

### Chats

- Layla conversations and owner handoffs.
- Cake requests captured from messages.
- Customer context and linked orders.

### Orders

- Customer, channel, fulfilment, payment, and status.
- Cake message or inscription.
- Filling and flavour choices.
- Design notes and allergen request.
- Pickup date and time.
- Deposit and remaining balance.

### Stock

- Raw ingredients, decorations, packaging, and finished goods.
- Supplier receipts with unit cost and use-by date.
- Recipes with yield and cost per unit.
- Production batches with output, inputs, production date, and use-by date.
- Reorder alerts and stock counts.
- Waste reasons: spoilage, prep trim, overproduction, unsold, expired, failed batch, and damage.
- Dated stock list sorted by earliest use-by date.

### Money

- Sales and gross profit.
- Food cost percentage.
- Prime cost percentage.
- Waste cost and stock variance.
- Actual usage compared with theoretical recipe usage.
- Supplier purchases and operating expenses.
- Delivery app commissions and custom cake materials.

### Customers

- Repeat buyers and order history.
- Custom cake preferences stored in conversations and orders.
- Open balances and recent contact.

### Settings

- Industry pack, currency, VAT, and stock policy.
- WhatsApp connection and business details remain in the existing settings flow.

## Bakery best practices represented

- Cost recipes from actual supplier prices.
- Use yield to calculate cost per cake or portion.
- Compare theoretical ingredient usage with actual usage.
- Record every waste event at cost.
- Separate raw materials from finished products.
- Produce small batches from demand and recent sales.
- Use reorder points for high-use ingredients.
- Mark received and produced lots with dates.
- Consume the earliest use-by lot first.
- Track unsold finished goods separately from preparation waste.
- Review top products by revenue, contribution, and food cost.
- Review sales by channel before changing production.

## Product boundary

Hasib controls owner-entered products, orders, stock movements, recipes, batches, waste, expenses, and dashboard calculations. It does not replace a POS, payroll, payments processor, accounting system, supplier integration, or food-safety certification record.
