# Construction — Oman/MENA operating pack

Audience: SME general contractors and fit-out firms. The design follows regional high-performer practices: early baseline discipline, integrated cost/schedule/procurement data, manager approvals, change-control cycle time, site productivity, quality close-out, cash collection and client satisfaction. Sources used for product direction include PwC Middle East's 2025 capital-project survey, Oman State Audit Institution's infrastructure-project toolkit, RICS's 2026 construction productivity report, and Saudi MOMRA schedule-quality guidance.

The live pack provides projects, weighted baselines, accepted progress, worker hours, categorized actual cost, estimate-to-complete, variations, commitments, claims, retention, aggregate site reports, quality/NCR, risks, structured client rating, operational tasks, audit history, role enforcement and the shared receivables/payment ledger.

Today shows only schedule-risk projects, submitted variation exposure and overdue certified receivables. Money & Insights exposes CPI/SPI and their PV/EV/AC values, forecast cost/variance/margin, variation, procurement, rework, quality, safety-frequency, productivity, billings/cash/collection/receivables/retention and experience measures with denominators and coverage. No universal benchmark is presented as a standard; targets are unset until 30 days of business-specific baseline data exists.

Excluded: BIM, drawings/files, payroll, personal injury/medical records, supplier payments, bank integration, formal accounting revenue recognition, tender portals, ERP/PMS integration, and autonomous commercial approval.
