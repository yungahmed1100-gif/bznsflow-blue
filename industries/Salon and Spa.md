- Booking requests

### Contacts

- Consent management

- Client history

### Appointments

- Appointment statuses

- Waitlist management

- Utilization reporting

### Operations

## Setup Panels

- Services
- Prices
- Staff records
- Provider schedules
- Rooms
- Membership records
- Gift cards
- Reminder templates

## Excluded Features

- External calendar control
- External booking control
- Payroll processing
- Payment processing
- Review platform management
- Medical records
- Automatic membership billing
- Multi-branch management

## Availability Rule

The Appointments tab appears only when BznsFlow owns the appointment record and availability logic. Until then, booking activity stays inside Chats as Booking Requests.
