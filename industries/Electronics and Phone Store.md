# Electronics And Phone Store Hasib Tabs

## Tabs And Functions

### Today

Shows what needs the owner’s attention:

- Layla order requests
- Customer handoffs
- Low-stock products
- Out-of-stock products
- Repairs ready
- Unpaid orders
- Today’s sales
- Best-selling product
- Setup progress

### Chats

Manages customer conversations:

- WhatsApp conversations
- Instagram conversations
- Layla responses
- Product questions
- Stock availability questions
- Price questions
- Order requests
- Repair requests
- Warranty questions
- Human handoffs

### Sales

Records store sales and payments:

- Product sales
- Customer orders
- IMEI selection
- Serial number selection
- Payment recording
- Deposits
- Refunds
- Exchanges
- Delivery status
- Outstanding balances
- Customer receipts

### Stock

Controls product inventory:

- Product catalog
- Product variants
- Models
- Storage options
- Colours
- New and used condition
- SKU tracking
- IMEI tracking
- Serial number tracking
- Stock receiving
- Stock adjustments
- Stock transfers
- Stock returns
- Damaged stock
- Written-off stock
- Reserved units
- Sold units
- Low-stock alerts
- Out-of-stock alerts
- Stock value
- Product cost
- Product margin
- Warranty status
- Warranty lookup
- Trade-in inventory
- Best-selling products
- Most-requested products
- Lost-demand products

### Service

Manages repairs and warranty work:

- Repair tickets
- Device details
- IMEI lookup
- Fault description
- Customer accessories
- Warranty verification
- Warranty provider
- Repair status
- Repair quote
- Labour charges
- Parts usage
- Repair deposits
- Ready-for-collection alerts
- Collected repairs
- Repair history

### Money

Shows financial performance:

- Sales revenue
- Revenue without VAT
- Cost of goods
- Gross profit
- Operating expenses
- Net profit
- Cash received
- Receivables
- Revenue by product
- Profit by product
- Best-selling products
- Product sales quantity
- Product sales value
- Product margin
- Trade-in costs
- Repair revenue
- Expense categories
- Accountant export

### Customers

Manages customer records:

- Customer contacts
- Purchase history
- Product ownership
- IMEI history
- Warranty history
- Repair history
- Trade-in history
- Conversation history
- Consent records
- Contact source
- Customer segments
- Import and export

### Settings

Controls the store workspace:

- WhatsApp connection
- Instagram connection
- Business profile
- Opening hours
- Business timezone
- Product catalog
- Stock policy
- Warranty settings
- Expense categories
- VAT settings
- Hasib industry setting

## Best Product Reporting

The Money and Today tabs must show:

- Best-selling product by quantity
- Best-selling product by revenue
- Most profitable product
- Best-selling product variant
- Fastest-selling product
- Slow-moving products
- Low-stock best sellers
- Out-of-stock demand
- Products customers requested
- Products customers did not buy
- Product margin ranking

Reports must separate models, variants, conditions, and serialised units where available.

## Hidden Or Excluded

- Payment processing
- BNPL processing
- E-invoice submission
- Payroll processing
- External POS hardware
- Automatic supplier purchasing
- External warranty-system control
- Multi-branch management

## Release Boundary

The electronics pack controls products, stock, sales, payments recorded by the owner, repairs, warranties, trade-ins, expenses, customer records, and reporting. External payment, supplier, warranty, accounting, and POS systems remain outside BznsFlow control.
