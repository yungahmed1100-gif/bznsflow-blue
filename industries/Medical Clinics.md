# Medical Clinics Hasib Tabs

## Tabs And Functions

### Today

Shows what needs the owner’s attention:

- Reception handoffs
- Pending service requests
- Layla activity
- Today’s recorded revenue
- Outstanding balances
- Setup progress

### Chats

Manages the reception inbox:

- WhatsApp conversations
- Layla responses
- Service questions
- Price questions
- Location questions
- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Branch preference
- Human handoffs
- Booking requests

Layla provides approved operational information and does not provide medical advice.

### Visits

Records operational visit activity:

- Recorded consultations
- Service charges
- Payment recording
- Outstanding balances
- Refund recording
- Visit status
- Patient history link

The existing Hasib Orders ledger remains the internal financial data source and displays as Visits.

### Services

Manages the clinic’s public service information:

- Consultation services
- Specialties
- Service prices
- Service categories
- Service descriptions
- Service availability
- Supplies inventory
- Low-stock supplies
- Service archive

### Money

Shows clinic financial performance:

- Revenue
- Cash received
- Receivables
- Expenses
- Net profit
- Revenue by service
- Expense categories
- Accountant export

### Patients

Manages operational contact information:

- Patient contact records
- Reception history
- Consent records
- Contact source
- Qualification status
- Last conversation
- Open conversation
- Import and export

Patients stores reception and booking details only.

### Settings

Controls the clinic workspace:

- WhatsApp connection
- Instagram connection
- Business profile
- Opening hours
- Business timezone
- Service setup
- Expense categories
- VAT settings
- Hasib industry setting

## Hidden Or Excluded

- Clinical records
- Diagnoses
- Symptoms
- Prescriptions
- Lab results
- X-rays
- Medical notes
- Treatment plans
- Insurance claims
- Payment processing
- Payroll
- External calendar control
- Automatic appointment booking

Appointment requests remain inside Chats until BznsFlow owns scheduling and passes the required health-data review.

## Release Boundary

The medical clinic pack remains behind release gates. It can manage approved reception information, service information, contact records, recorded financial activity, and reporting. It cannot store or process clinical information.
