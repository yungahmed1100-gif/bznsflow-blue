# Automotive workshop — Oman/MENA operating pack

Audience: independent multi-brand workshops in Oman, with one manager and up to five verified service advisors or technicians. The pack follows the operating pattern used by strong workshop performers: appointment capacity by bay and technician, vehicle history, digital inspection, immutable estimate revisions, recorded customer approval, technician clocks, parts allocation, a final quality gate, one linked charge and post-service experience.

Today deliberately shows only three exceptions: estimates awaiting approval, vehicles past their promised time, and vehicles ready for collection. Workshop covers appointments, vehicles, work orders, estimates, approvals, labor and quality. Parts & supplies reuses the stock ledger at the legacy `?tab=stock&view=products` URL. Money & Insights reports response, booking, estimate approval, efficiency, on-time delivery, first-time fix, average repair order, revenue, cash, receivables and experience with explicit coverage. Targets remain unset until the workshop has a 30-day baseline.

Technicians are controlled employee accounts inside the existing five-person limit. They can see assigned vehicles and work, record inspections, operate their own labor clock and handle assigned parts. They cannot browse the customer directory, other technicians' work, estimates, aggregate Money, team or settings. Managers and service advisors retain customer, estimate and collection controls; managers alone receive aggregate reporting and administration.

Excluded from this release: diagnosis automation, safety-critical repair advice, insurer claims, OEM warranty adjudication, government registration, accounting revenue recognition, supplier payments, payroll, telematics, high-voltage certification claims and external DMS/PMS/calendar synchronization.

Product direction references [NADA dealership workforce and operations research](https://www.nada.org/nada/research-data), [J.D. Power customer-service research](https://www.jdpower.com/business/automotive), and regional automotive aftermarket analysis from [Roland Berger](https://www.rolandberger.com/en/Insights/Global-Topics/Automotive-Industry/). These are contextual sources, not universal KPI targets for an Omani workshop.

Status: implemented and locally verified as a preview-only release candidate. `automotive` remains outside `HASIB_LIVE_PACKS`; production activation requires explicit owner authorization after final deployment preflight.
